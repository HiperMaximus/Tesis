# Goal

Produce the UIS undergraduate thesis provisionally titled:

> Modelo semi-supervisado de redes neuronales convolucionales equivariantes
> para la segmentación y clasificación de imágenes histopatológicas

The thesis evaluates whether representations learned with continuous
`SO(2)` equivariance are useful under limited annotation in digital pathology.
The final argument must connect:

1. reconstruction and representation learning;
2. WSI-level classification;
3. WSI segmentation at patch-grid resolution under sparse labels;
4. latent-space response to continuous rotations;
5. limitations and mixed evidence relative to the normal baseline.

`plan/` preserves the approved Tesis I proposal. `thesis/` is the only active
final-document source. Accepted experimental evidence comes from
`../equivariant-vae`.
